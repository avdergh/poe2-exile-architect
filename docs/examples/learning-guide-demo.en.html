03

Explain failure cases and unknowns

Clear boundaries help a player decide when to change their actions or investigate further.

  • Can the required condition start without adds?
  • How does the resume after movement or interruption?
  • Which conclusions are source facts, and which remain inferences?

Keep in context

This demo includes no PoB measurements. A real guide should identify the skill, state, and version behind its readings and preserve unresolved questions.
