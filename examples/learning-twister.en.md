## Opening excerpt

> Whirling Slash handles preparation; Twister turns that preparation into hits. This guide starts with one complete sequence, then explains how the two weapon sets, gems, equipment, passives, and ascendancy work together. First build an overall picture of the playstyle, then use the component explanations to understand each choice. Navigation and bookmarks let you return to a specific part.

## Start with one attack

The guide breaks the sequence into three actions:

1. **Prepare the whirlwind**: use Whirling Slash to establish and build up Whirlwind.
2. **Empower the throw**: make Twister interact with the prepared object while it is still present.
3. **Maintain contact**: make the empowered whirlwind actually reach the target, accounting for movement.

| Build choice | Its primary role |
| --- | --- |
| Whirling Slash and speed investment | Reduce preparation time and create the object Twister uses |
| Twister and elemental damage investment | Turn the prepared empowerment into hits |
| Charge skills and recovery | Keep successive preparation and attack cycles going |
| Evasion and Energy Shield | Create room to complete actions and re-establish the setup |

> The prepared object must exist, the skill interaction must succeed, and the whirlwind must reach the target. If any of these conditions fails, raising paper damage alone cannot recover the lost output.

## How the guide develops the explanation

Later chapters cover weapon switching, damage scaling, support roles, charge generation and consumption, persistent buffs, defenses, equipment, and passives. The final chapter considers bosses without adds.
